Industry

SBE Flow Control provides high-performance engineering solutions for the pulp and paper industry, combining proven technology and application expertise to maximize reliability, optimize process efficiency, reduce downtime, and drive sustainable plant performance.

The pulp and paper industry demands reliable, high-performance process equipment that can support continuous production, optimize operational efficiency, and meet increasingly stringent environmental requirements. SBE Flow Control provides advanced engineering solutions designed to address the critical mechanical, flow-control and condition-monitoring requirements of modern pulp and paper manufacturing facilities. With extensive application expertise, SBE Flow Control offers a comprehensive portfolio that includes mechanical seals for pumps and compressors, industrial valves, and advanced machinery condition-monitoring technologies. These solutions are engineered to perform reliably in demanding process environments, helping protect critical equipment and maintain consistent plant performance. By combining proven technologies with application-focused engineering support, SBE Flow Control helps pulp and paper manufacturers enhance equipment reliability, minimize unplanned downtime, reduce maintenance requirements, and improve overall plant efficiency. Our solutions also support customers in achieving more sustainable operations by contributing to improved energy efficiency, reduced resource consumption, and optimized process performance. SBE Flow Control is committed to delivering dependable solutions that help pulp and paper producers maintain continuous operations while meeting the evolving demands of productivity, reliability, safety, and environmental sustainability.

Detection, Safety and Protection

We provides advanced gas detection and safety equipment for reliable monitoring of toxic and combustible gases, vapours, and oxygen. Our solutions include area gas monitors, portable detectors, face masks, and personal protection equipment for safer workplaces. H₂S dangers also pose as a problem in the pulp and paper industry. That’s why our safety products focus on H₂S detection and monitoring, incident prevention and rescue preparedness with H₂S respirators.
